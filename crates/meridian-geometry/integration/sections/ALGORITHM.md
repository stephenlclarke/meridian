## Construction and proof obligations

For an original source facet F, let d(v) = n dot v - offset, using its unchanged unnormalized outward normal. Consider the parallel plane d(x) = epsilon for positive epsilon tending to zero. The projection drops F's dominant normal axis and is invertible on every parallel plane. The tested projected sample stays fixed; its lifted displacement has positive dot product with n.

An edge crosses the shifted plane for sufficiently small epsilon exactly when one endpoint has d > 0 and the other has d <= 0. Evaluate its limiting intersection on d = 0 with the exact original line interpolation. This is a symbolic one-sided limit, with no finite epsilon, tolerance or floating-point approximation. Each triangle contributes zero or two endpoints. Retain their segment even when the endpoints coincide. An entirely coplanar triangle contributes no shifted section. Two zero vertices and a positive third vertex contribute their common zero edge; a negative third vertex contributes none. A single zero vertex with two positive vertices contributes a point. Do not deduplicate coincident segments: two tangent triangles can contribute the same edge, and their two crossings must cancel.

For every possible original owner, collect its complete section through the infinite source plane. The owner box need only meet the closed source-facet box. The cached triangle tree is queried against the plane, rather than the finite facet box, so remote parts of a loop remain present when an owner completely encloses F. Exact box support bounds reject a node only when its minimum distance is positive or its maximum distance is nonpositive. Every triangle that crosses a sufficiently small positive shifted plane therefore survives. Traversal preserves original ordinal order. Nonconvex and disconnected closed owners need no convex approximation; cavities contribute their own boundary loops to the same owner parity.

Closed reciprocal source sheets pass the unchanged original topology and sheet-crossing audits before this construction. Away from section boundaries, even/odd crossings classify their solid using complete closed sections. The half-open y predicate counts each section vertex once. An exact orientation decides whether a crossing lies to the sample's right, without constructing a new quotient coordinate. Horizontal edges and endpoint equality retain explicit boundary ambiguity. Distinct owners combine by logical union, never by summing or averaging fields.

The unchanged physical arrangement already contains every finite original triangle/plane intersection meeting F. Atomic samples lie strictly inside noded slabs and bands: their x lies between consecutive endpoint/intersection events, and their y lies between consecutive active physical levels. They cannot lie on a nonvertical physical segment, vertical contact, endpoint or isolated physical point. Every limiting section boundary is a subset of these original intersections wherever it meets F. Thus the limiting section's interior membership is stable near each ordinary sample. The projected-axis displacement and the parent outward-facing generic ray both enter the same local open side of every noncoplanar source surface. Coplanar surfaces have parallel normals, so both displacements choose the same side. This establishes the classification equivalence obligation without assuming convex owners.

Any boundary ambiguity still delegates to the unchanged parent generic-ray policy, including retries and individual triangle checks. No new rejection or contact shortcut substitutes for it. Co-oriented coplanar exposed facets use the original complete style and affine UV agreement and smallest source identity. Opposed shared faces are classified as occupied by the adjacent side-triangle section loop. Output geometry, source ownership and UV interpolation never come from the new section segments.
