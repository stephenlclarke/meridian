# Ox Mk II source evidence

SH100 preserves its original sand-graphite artwork in the 688-face
detailed mesh and 376-face native mesh. Its archived calibration is
2,200,000/82,469 metres per source unit, and its finite empty-dry
reference records 515,013 kg. The runtime catalogue records flight as
unavailable and purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design contract
specifies player flight and blueprint fabrication.

Each original contains 12 closed components. Both preserve the same 13
measured overlap pairs, including each deck against both braces and the
cockpit glazing against its crossmember. The freight deck bodies have
at least 7,469 mm of vertical separation. The glazing is at least 426 mm
above the upper deck body. Each luminous recess lies at least 133 mm aft
of its casing.

The 12 supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guards provide 2,123–4,780 mm
of clearance, including 2,123 mm through the centre service gap and
3,586 mm along either forward offset route. Every native vertex lies
on the detailed surface. The most distant detailed vertex sits about
277 mm from the native surface.

The source and the signed Studio definition use Ox Mk II's own geometry
and calibration. Both Developer ID–signed offline Studio runs passed
all seven probes with the 688-face and 376-face originals and produced
identical exports.
