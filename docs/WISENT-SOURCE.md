# Wisent source evidence

SH096 preserves the original ochre-charcoal artwork in its 2,600-face
detailed mesh and 816-face native mesh, calibrated at 152/9 metres per
source unit. Its finite empty-dry reference records 227,786 kg. The
runtime catalogue records flight as unavailable and purchase pending
fabrication. The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint
fabrication.

The historical recipe produces a separate 488-face inspection candidate.
It retains all 26 native parts, including eight blind service wells, two
cross bridges, all six dock-frame pieces and both aft drive assemblies.
Nineteen parts retain their exact original vertices, faces, materials and
UVs. Both cargo bays retain all five longitudinal stations and at least
7,836 mm of lateral body separation. All 35 measured native overlaps
reproduce, and the separate candidate survey found the same 35 pairs.

The maximum surface distance from the archived native mesh to the
candidate is bounded at 1,418–1,443 mm, with 1,355–1,380 mm in reverse.
From the detailed mesh to the candidate, the maximum is 2,907–2,928 mm
near a detailed port service well. The corresponding detailed-to-archive
maximum is 1,743–1,766 mm. The two original meshes remain separate visual
streams.

The 46 supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guards provide 1,523–6,734 mm
of clearance, including 1,523 mm through the dock aperture and 1,915 mm
along the central freight-bay lane. Both Developer ID–signed Studio runs
used the original detailed mesh and the candidate, passed all seven
probes and produced byte-identical exports.
