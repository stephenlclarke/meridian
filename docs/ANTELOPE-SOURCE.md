# Antelope source evidence

SH089 preserves 2,672 detailed and 912 native original triangles at
20,000,000/1,533,783 metres per source unit. The finite empty-dry
reference records 50,994 kg. The original catalogue records player
flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design
contract prescribes player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The pinned family recipe produces a separate 504-face inspection
candidate with all 16 parts. Nine parts retain exact vertices, face
order, materials and UVs. The open arc retains both caps and its bow
gap. All 14 measured original overlaps reproduce. The candidate
arc volume is 669 m³, compared with 972 m³ in the archived
native mesh. Conservative distance bounds between the candidate and
archived arcs are 1,245 mm from candidate to archive and
1,417 mm in the reverse direction.

Thirty-seven inspection supports contain all three meshes. Twelve
local spans cover the open arc with 264 shared seam vertices. The
supports use 20 mm rounding. The guarded bow route clears by
2,422 mm, and the six interior apertures clear by
190–241 mm. The independent candidate aperture
proof has a minimum clearance of 499 mm.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match. All
seven offline probes passed. The signed artwork uses the original
detail mesh and separate candidate with arc seam vertices duplicated
so each local support preserves its portion of the original surface.
