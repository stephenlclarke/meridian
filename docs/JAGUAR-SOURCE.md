# Jaguar source evidence

SH081 preserves its original artwork in metres. The detailed mesh has
5,820 triangles and measures 50 m wide, 30.58 m tall and 94 m long.
The native mesh has 432 triangles. The finite empty-dry source records
187,775 kg. The original catalogue and R3 design contract both mark
Jaguar as unavailable for player flight and purchase.

The inspection envelope uses 34 local supports with 20 mm rounding.
Conservative stand-off upper bounds are 2,991 mm against the detailed
mesh and 4,741 mm against the native mesh. Detailed vertex 2909 is more
than 4,143 mm from every native triangle.

Both meshes preserve 14 structural overlaps and four flush aft pod
and nozzle contacts at z = −42 m. The overlaps connect the four thrust
arms, both forward outriggers, the dorsal deckhouse and the canopy.
The detailed canopy ridge also overlaps the canopy. Guarded clearance
is 1,619 mm on the dorsal route and ranges from 1,556 to 3,979 mm on
the six lateral and inner aft routes.

The two Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match.
All seven offline artwork probes passed.
