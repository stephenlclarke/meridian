# G03 Bonded Freight Passage

At a main orbital, press **5** for Equipment. Press **]** until **G03 TOREN bonded freight** appears. Select the premium tier with the arrow keys and press **Enter** to review its route and price. **B** goes back. Confirm with **Enter** to pay the 75 CR bond and load three original Machinery tonnes. You need three free tonnes, ordinary fuel and a free freight contract slot. Finish or abandon any G02 Consented Freight job first.

The fee is 480 CR plus 95 CR per quoted leg and 80 CR per premium tier. TOREN sets tiers H0, H1 and H2. There is no deadline. With a full tank and stock fit, Avenor's first H0 offer goes to Renas for 575 CR. H1 also goes to Renas for 655 CR. H2 goes through Renas to Lareria for 830 CR.

Open the chart with **1**, select the next quoted stop, review the direct route and confirm. Launch, fly the route and dock at the destination main orbital. At the TOREN workshop, select **Receive one original tonne** or **Receive all remaining original tonnes**. Review and confirm. Each receipt frees its cargo space. The third tonne pays the full fee and returns the 75 CR bond. For H0 at Renas, a commander who starts with 720 CR has 645 CR after issue and 1,295 CR after final reception, before other purchases. The first completed job adds the Bonded Freight Family Trace to **Shift+J**.

To abandon, return to the original sender and confirm **Return consignment at sender**. TOREN takes the remaining originals and retains the bond. Cold Continue restores the individual tonnes, their workshop receipts and the account. Cargo lost with the ship earns no fee, refund or clue.

## Source and route coverage

The Original board retains accepted terms and advances its offer revision from the issuer's job history. The manifest declares an hourly refresh. The source reservation policy uses retained jobs to set the revision for this family. Each receipt requires a physical workshop entry with a later dock sequence and visit than the issue. Market Machinery stays separate.

This build generates routes between the admitted Avenor, Renas and Lareria main orbitals. The legacy controlled Avlanar–Sedor H0 flight covers a different pair of orbitals and remains outside this build's playable route graph.
