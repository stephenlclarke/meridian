# Falconet source evidence

SH057 preserves the original 3,108-triangle detailed mesh and 184-triangle native
mesh, both in metres. The detailed bounds measure 16.60 m wide, 7.234 m tall and
19.59 m long. The finite empty-dry reference is
`finite-intrinsic-empty-falconet` at 13,602 kg. The native mesh meets the original
512-triangle cap with its original coordinates, faces and appearance.

The inspection envelope has 25 local support groups with 20 mm rounding. Nested
pod surfaces share only their own pod group. Conservative stand-off upper
bounds are 1,555 mm from the detailed mesh and 2,783 mm from the native mesh. An
exact source vertex witness gives a strict detailed-to-native lower bound of
1,883 mm.

Seven exact swept-triangle proofs certify guarded clearances of 559–879 mm in the
ventral, forward-rail, aft-strut, and pod-to-rail passages. Point-in-solid proofs
establish seven overlapping joints in both original meshes. These include both
forward struts, rails, and aft struts, as well as the canopy joint.

Developer ID–signed Hull Studio produced identical offline artwork exports and
responses for the original 3,108/184 definition. The source verifier supplies
the clearance and joint evidence. The current catalogue lists the hull as
unflyable and unavailable for normal purchase. The R3 contract records blueprint
fabrication as the intended purchase policy.
