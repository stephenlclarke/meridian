# Griffon Spectre source evidence

SH073 keeps its original 1,644-triangle detailed and 596-triangle native
visible meshes. The archived calibration is 1,200,000/132,799 metres per
source unit. Both original meshes measure 23.57 m wide, 8.46 m tall and
30.00 m long. The finite empty-dry source records 28,784 kg.

The pinned creator-family recipe produced a 380-triangle candidate for
offline inspection. Both frozen tests passed. The candidate preserves all
19 parts, including 13 with exact geometry, materials and UVs. Its height
extrema move inward by up to 180 mm at the bottom and 154 mm at the top.
The width and length extrema are preserved. The 596-triangle archive
remains the visible native source.

Conservative distance bounds from candidate to original native and back
are 998 and 842 mm. Against the detailed surface and back they are 998 and
1,740 mm. The 29 inspection supports have upper stand-off bounds of
1,561 mm against detailed and 1,573 mm against both the original native
and generated candidate.

All seven guarded numerical routes return the same results with the
original meshes and the generated candidate, with clearances from 995
to 1,558 mm. Both inner gates retain 1,134 mm. The paths above and below
the forward bridge retain 995 and 1,425 mm. Seven overlap witnesses pass
across all three meshes.

Both Developer ID–signed offline Studio exports and responses match, and all
seven artwork probes passed. Gameplay qualification remains open.
