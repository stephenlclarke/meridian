## Cold continuation

The original restore explicitly clears `activeECMActivation` and live ownership/events while retaining the remaining duration. Meridian follows that distinction. Continue retains the timer, paid activation history, resource debit and already terminated missiles; it clears live permission to destroy any newly launched missile. `EcmSnapshot::remaining_ticks` can therefore be positive while `field` is `None`. Restore does not emit an activation, spend energy or renew a field.

A retained first-restore boundary records the physical/world clocks and accepted attack watermark where live permission ended. Historical effect validation still requires the original paid fit, emitter, activation ordinal and exact missile ending. A real missile launched after that boundary survives the time-only timer. Manual reactivation refuses until the remaining 32-tick interval expires, then requires another actual command and another 22-energy debit. Pause freezes the timer. Travel changes visit and cannot carry a field into another scene. Repeated Continue does not renew permission or extend the timer.

The existing current-format save content fingerprint changes. Old content refuses under the repository's current no-migration policy; no original player save or earlier installed preview is rewritten. The activation history admits at most 128 entries, subject to the unchanged whole-save budget. That is a Meridian proof-capacity guard, not an original hardware charge or price rule.
