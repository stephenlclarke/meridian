# Elk source evidence

SH087 preserves 1,584 detailed and 536 native original triangles at
20,000/1,731 metres per source unit. Its finite empty-dry reference
records 51,639 kg. The original runtime catalogue records flight
as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design
contract prescribes player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The pinned recipe produces a separate 464-face inspection candidate
with all 14 original parts. Eight parts retain exact vertices, faces,
materials and texture mappings. The candidate retains all nine
measured original overlaps, including the hull-to-bridge and
bridge-to-glazing joints. Conservative surface distance bounds
between candidate and archived native are 997 mm in either direction.

The 24 inspection supports contain all three meshes with 20 mm rounding.
Conservative stand-off upper bounds are 2,448 mm against the detailed
original, 2,559 mm against the native original, and 2,861 mm against
the candidate. Seven guarded routes reproduce with 271–1,620 mm of
clearance. The 271 mm guard covers the route between the aft drives.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and responses match. All seven
offline probes passed with the original detailed mesh and the candidate.
