and upper interdeck paths retain 1,746 and 1,664 mm. The original support
bounds establish at least 3,674 mm of source separation between the
service wells and the decks above them, and at least 571 mm between the
platforms and the vertical side braces. Nine overlap witnesses replay in
both originals, including all three service-well joints.

seven offline artwork probes passed. Gameplay qualification remains open.
