from candidate shield to archived shield and 1,120 mm in the reverse

including eight shield spans with 192 shared seam vertices. The supports
use 20 mm rounding. They bound stand-off at 2,580 mm for the detailed
original, 2,772 mm for the native original, and 2,984 mm for the candidate.

the dorsal route clears by 1,683 mm.

Both signed offline Studio exports match and all seven probes passed.
Studio uses the original detail and the separate 508-face candidate.
Its duplicated seam vertices preserve the ordered coordinates of every
triangle and allow both sides of each aperture to remain open.
