# Barbary source evidence

SH059 preserves the original 3,852-triangle detailed mesh and 192-triangle native
mesh, both in metres. The detailed bounds measure 24.60 m wide, 8.72 m tall and
26.00 m long. The finite empty-dry reference is
`finite-intrinsic-empty-barbary` at 25,117 kg. The native mesh meets the
512-triangle limit with its original geometry and appearance.

The inspection envelope uses 22 local support groups with 20 mm rounding. The
conservative stand-off upper bounds are 2,890 mm from the detailed mesh and
2,881 mm from the native mesh. For the intact wing support the corresponding
bounds are 2,595 mm and 2,811 mm. An exact source vertex witness gives a strict
detailed-to-native lower bound of 1,272 mm.

Seven source sweeps certify guarded clearances of 609–1,609 mm below the center,
above and below both outboard wing sections, and above both aft pods. Exact
point-in-solid proofs establish eight overlapping joints in both original
meshes. Those include the canopy and wing joints to the central hull, the
outboard struts, and the joints between the aft pods and wing.

Developer ID–signed Hull Studio produced two byte-identical offline artwork
exports and responses for the 3,852/192 definition. Exact source replay supplies
the numeric clearance and joint evidence. The runtime catalogue lists Barbary
as unflyable and unavailable for normal purchase. The original R3 contract
records blueprint fabrication as the intended purchase policy.
