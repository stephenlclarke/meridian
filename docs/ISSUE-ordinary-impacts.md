# Ordinary station impacts must fail safe gunnery

The base clips a Pulse beam at the actual station surface, but records no original ordinary impact or legal effect. An attempt with three practice hits can therefore retain readiness after striking an ordinary actor. This blocks faithful finite-missile collision outcomes too.

The immutable original records actual owner/emission/victim/physical contact evidence, applies the station-attack law window and keeps `ordinaryHit` as a permanent success refusal for that T02 attempt. Source capacities, station susceptibility and the shared player resource rules must also be retained without a host-supplied damage command.

Acceptance requires a real public station strike, permanent failure through quiet time and Continue, harmless original beacon contacts, once-only/current-identity impact application, source law timing, and refusal of unsupported ordnance save state. Existing paid property and old supported saves must survive. Full source rules, regression criteria and the missile successor boundary are in [ORDINARY-IMPACTS.md](ORDINARY-IMPACTS.md).
