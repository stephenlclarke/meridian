# Shikra Interdictor source evidence

SH095 preserves its 5,396-face detailed and 1,404-face native originals
with cobalt-cream artwork and calibration of 3,000,000/324,991 metres per
source unit. Its finite empty-dry reference records 37,940 kg. The runtime
catalogue records flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication.
The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The separate 512-face inspection candidate retains all 41 native parts,
including the 20 blind service-well parts. It keeps eleven parts
byte-identical and selects original SH095 vertices for the other 30. It
preserves the original native extrema on all three axes and each part's
material inventory. The rebuilt surfaces approximate the original shapes
and UV mappings. Both original visual streams preserve the exact original
geometry, materials and UVs.

All 18 measured native overlaps survive at their original witness points,
including the four diagonal spars and the cockpit crossmember. The
candidate survey observed the same overlap pairs. The original and
candidate retain at least 46 mm of longitudinal separation between each
aft casing and its luminous recess. Conservative surface-distance upper
bounds are 1,228 mm from the archived native mesh to the candidate and
996 mm in reverse.

The 91 local supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guarded routes reproduce
with 359–2,573 mm of clearance, including both openings between diagonal
spars. Both Developer ID–signed Studio runs used the original detailed
mesh and the 512-face candidate, passed all seven probes and produced
byte-identical exports.
