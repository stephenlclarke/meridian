# Anoa source evidence

SH098 preserves the original forest-silver artwork in its 6,720-face
detailed and 1,928-face native meshes. Their calibration is 190/9 metres
per source unit. The finite empty-dry reference records 442,139 kg. The
runtime catalogue records flight as unavailable and purchase pending
fabrication. The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint
fabrication.

The separate 512-face inspection candidate retains all 34 native parts.
Seven parts are byte-identical, including the radial supports and cockpit
crossmember. Each of the two collars remains a closed genus-one mesh.
Both collars use eight angular sections with three original vertices per
section. The other resampled parts also use original vertices. Candidate
faces approximate the source surfaces and their texture mappings. The
original visual streams keep the authored artwork intact.

All 40 measured native overlaps reproduce at common witnesses, and a
separate survey observed the same pairs. The 88 supports include eight
local spans per collar with 368 dual memberships across the three meshes.
Both axial collar routes retain at least 1,656 mm clearance. All seven
guards reproduce with 1,656–3,469 mm clearance.

The maximum archived-native-to-candidate surface distance is bounded at
1,864–1,887 mm, and the reverse direction at 1,730–1,754 mm. An earlier
allocation reserved more triangles for the collars and had a 4,277–4,278
mm archived-native-to-candidate maximum at a silo. The selected candidate
has a 3,533–3,534 mm detailed-to-candidate maximum near a detailed service
well. The detailed-to-archive maximum is 2,358–2,382 mm.

Both Developer ID–signed offline Studio runs used the original detailed
mesh and the separate candidate. They passed all seven probes and
produced byte-identical exports.
