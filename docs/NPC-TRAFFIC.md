## Beams, damage and security

Pirates pursue actual live cargo carriers, flee at less than one-quarter hull energy and never derive contact from an AI intent or UI target. Civilian flight reacts to actual recorded aggression. NPCs have explicitly generated standard front Pulse stock and their own SH025 energy/shields/heat/cooldown. The reference capacities are 380 energy and 300 per shield, with 4.2/1.8 recharge per second. Actual fire spends one energy/ten heat, requires opening heat at most 230 and 18 ticks between discharges. A Pulse hit deals 15 units through the actual struck facing before hull energy. These are project simulation units, not asserted SI calibration or newly qualified mass properties.

The native SH025 mesh supplies first-contact rays. Station, player, peers and current missile/celestial occlusion can suppress an NPC shot. A shot is retained only after its actual surface query succeeds, with typed source/victim IDs, world/local ticks, origin, direction, struck pose, point and facing. Shield absorption, energy spending/recharge, heat/cooling and overkill conserve against the bounded hit history. Up to 128 beam receipts are retained; proof is not dropped or rewritten. Automatic NPC fire stops at that bound, and a new player NPC hit refuses before a discharge when no receipt can be retained.

The player's real fitted front Pulse can hit the actual nearest NPC surface through the existing World first-contact/resource path. Its actual item/emission is bound before candidate NPC damage commits with the discharge. Stale, paused, invalid-input and failed admission paths preserve live bytes. Active T02 NPC firing is explicitly refused until its ordinary-actor incident receipt is integrated; NPCs are never relabelled as a station to fabricate that proof.

Hits on a clean trader are retained as verified security incidents. The security projection names **SH031 required/unavailable**; it does not mint a police body, police damage, an unwitnessed fine or an invisible interceptor. The first bounded NPC-AI combat path is pirate versus trader. NPC-to-player shield damage, complete NPC missile/projectile hit replay/targeting, original NPC/T02 incident/legal integration, real police deployment, bounties, salvage and the remaining controllers are still open. Missile occlusion of NPC beams does not establish the reciprocal NPC collision/damage/replay path for flying missiles. These joins are required before broader NPC gameplay acceptance.
