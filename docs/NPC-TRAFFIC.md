## World ownership and actual bodies

One World owns the whole traffic state. The typed `NpcId` contains profile, actual system and a permanent lifetime serial. It is descriptive, never a hit, custody or spawn capability. No public constructor, spawn override or host-submitted damage/price/success flag exists. An ID becomes an actor only when its actual source and clearance checks succeed. Completed and destroyed identities remain recorded and cannot be rerolled, reused or regenerated by Continue.

`World::traffic_snapshot()` returns small immutable actor poses/resources/manifests, the retained latest beam, verified clean-trader hit count and explicit population gates. Active actors identify **SH025** and use the same station-space frame and existing bound SH025 render/native collision data. The renderer must reuse that actual asset and pose; it must not draw proxy primitives, another ship family or unsupported-role replacements. Complete actors are berthed; destroyed actors retain their native solid hull until a genuine later wreck/custody mechanism exists. The minimal App delta only handles the new contact messages. It does not render NPCs. Do not activate invisible physical actors in a shipping build.

At most four lifetime actors exist: one trader and, for one quarter of deterministic seeds, one pirate in each of Avenor and Renas. Local active flight ticks drive births at 480 and 900 ticks. The fixed source requests wait if actual launch/player/peer/celestial clearance or finite stock refuses them; no identity/payment is assumed. Inactive systems, docked or paused time, jump spooling and active existing freight/tutorial protection do not simulate unobserved traffic journeys. No actor per entity, background runtime, filesystem, GPU, wall clock or new random dependency enters core.

## Beams, damage and security

Pirates pursue actual live cargo carriers, flee at less than one-quarter hull energy and never derive contact from an AI intent or UI target. Civilian flight reacts to actual recorded aggression. NPCs have explicitly generated standard front Pulse stock and their own SH025 energy/shields/heat/cooldown. The reference capacities are 380 energy and 300 per shield, with 4.2/1.8 recharge per second. Actual fire spends one energy/ten heat, requires opening heat at most 230 and 18 ticks between discharges. A Pulse hit deals 15 units through the actual struck facing before hull energy. These are project simulation units, not asserted SI calibration or newly qualified mass properties.

The native SH025 mesh supplies first-contact rays. Station, actual player/peer bodies and the staged same-frame missile/celestial scene can suppress an NPC shot. A shot is retained only after its actual surface query succeeds, with typed source/victim IDs, world/local ticks, origin, direction, struck pose, point and facing. Shield absorption, energy spending/recharge, heat/cooling and overkill conserve against the bounded hit history. Up to 128 combined NPC-victim beams and distinct player-hit receipts are retained; proof is not dropped or rewritten. Automatic NPC fire stops at that bound, and a new player NPC hit refuses before a discharge when no receipt can be retained.

The player's real fitted front Pulse can hit the actual nearest NPC surface through the existing World first-contact/resource path. Its actual item/emission is bound before candidate NPC damage commits with the discharge. Stale, paused, invalid-input and failed admission paths preserve live bytes. Active T02 NPC firing is explicitly refused until its ordinary-actor incident receipt is integrated; NPCs are never relabelled as a station to fabricate that proof.

Hits on a clean trader are retained as verified security incidents. The security projection names **SH031 required/unavailable**; it does not mint a police body, police damage, an unwitnessed fine or an invisible interceptor. Pirates can pursue the actual nearer player cargo carrier or retaliate against a retained real player Pulse hit. A copied UI identity or empty hold creates no attack authority. An admitted player contact debits the actual pirate energy/heat/cooldown once and stages 15 damage through the actual player facing before reducing real player hull energy. It does not increment player emissions/incidents or manufacture a station impact, fine, bounty or police body.
