and 448 native triangles. The detailed mesh measures 28.28 m wide, 22.164 m
tall and 52.61 m long. The finite empty-dry source records 88,810 kg.

The numerical envelope uses 56 local supports with 20 mm rounding. Eight
overlapping spans follow each collar. A total of 512 seam vertices belong to
two spans, and every source triangle belongs to at least one support. The
detailed stand-off upper bound improves from 5,060 to 3,727 mm. The native
bound remains 5,096 mm. A vertex on the original detailed port float is
more than 2,190 mm from every native triangle.

Seven guarded numerical routes clear by 1,148–3,263 mm after splitting the
collars. The central path between the floats retains 1,484 mm. The ventral
path improves from 1,484 to 3,263 mm. Six joints, including the aft dorsal
section, and all four occupied collar centres are verified in both originals.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match, and all
seven original artwork probes passed. Studio uses the original 28 artwork
groups. Gameplay qualification remains open.
