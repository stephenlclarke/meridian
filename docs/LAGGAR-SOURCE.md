# Laggar source evidence

SH085 preserves its original 3,108 detailed and 184 native triangles
in metres. The detailed mesh measures 27 m wide, 8.9 m tall and
38.59 m long. Its finite empty-dry reference records 24,443 kg.
The original catalogue records player flight as unavailable and
purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design contract prescribes
player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The 25 inspection supports use 20 mm rounding. Conservative stand-off
upper bounds are 2,381 mm against the detailed mesh and 3,080 mm
against the native mesh. Detailed vertex 1566 is more than 2,338 mm
from every native triangle. Seven guarded routes reproduce with
1,238–4,979 mm of clearance, including 1,243 mm on each inner aft route.

Both meshes preserve seven overlaps. The canopy overlaps the hull,
each outrigger arm overlaps the hull and its pod, and each nozzle
overlaps its pod. The detailed canopy ridge overlaps the canopy.
In both originals, each fin clears its arm by at least 799 mm,
its pod by at least 849 mm, and the hull by at least 1,149 mm.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match.
All seven offline artwork probes passed.
