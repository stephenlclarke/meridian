maximum lies between 2,406 and 2,431 mm at an outer detailed service well.
The reverse maximum lies between 1,455 and 1,477 mm.

The 68 local inspection supports cover all three meshes with 20 mm
rounding. The seven shared route guards clear by 711–1,813 mm. Both
signed Studio exports and raw responses match. All seven offline
