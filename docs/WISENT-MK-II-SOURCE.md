# Wisent Mk II source evidence

SH097 preserves the original ochre-charcoal artwork in its 2,600-face
detailed mesh and 816-face native mesh at 30,400/1,863 metres per source
unit. The finite empty-dry reference records 220,975 kg. The runtime
catalogue records flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication.
The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The historical recipe produces a separate 488-face inspection candidate.
It retains all 26 native parts, including eight blind service wells, both
cross bridges, all six dock-frame pieces and both aft drive assemblies.
Nineteen parts retain their exact original vertices, faces, materials and
UVs. Both cargo bays retain all five longitudinal stations and at least
7,760 mm of lateral body separation. All 35 measured native overlaps
reproduce. The independent candidate survey found the same pairs.

The maximum surface distance from the archived native mesh to the
candidate is bounded at 1,404–1,430 mm, with 1,341–1,367 mm in reverse.
The detailed-to-candidate maximum is 2,816–2,821 mm near a detailed
starboard service well. The corresponding detailed-to-archive maximum
is 1,670–1,690 mm. Both original meshes remain separate visual streams.

Forty-six supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guards provide 1,469–6,994 mm
of clearance, including 1,469 mm through the dock aperture and 1,901 mm
along the central freight-bay lane. Both Developer ID–signed Studio runs
used the original detailed mesh and the candidate, passed all seven probes
and produced identical exports.
