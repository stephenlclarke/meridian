# Giraffe Liner source evidence

SH093 preserves both pearl-teal originals at one metre per source unit.
The detailed mesh has 3,492 triangles and 30 closed components. The native
mesh has 216 triangles and 11 closed components. The archive records
72,102 kg for the finite empty-dry reference. The runtime catalogue records
flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design
contract specifies player flight and blueprint fabrication.

Both original meshes retain the forward cabin-to-canopy overlap and two
gallery-to-rail overlaps. The detailed mesh also has overlaps between the
gallery struts and the pressure hull. The equivalent native struts have
at least 878 mm of separation from the hull. The struts have at least
450 mm of separation from the galleries in both originals. The two
galleries themselves have at least 5,400 mm of lateral separation.
At their common aft plane, the native drive caps each have at least
551 mm of lateral clearance from the pressure hull cap.

The 26 rounded supports use 20 mm of padding. Their conservative
stand-off upper bounds are 2,381 mm from the detailed mesh and 3,539 mm
from the native mesh. One detailed vertex is more than 2,626 mm
from the native surface. Seven guards provide 729–2,894 mm of clearance,
including 2,078 mm between the observation galleries.

The two Developer ID–signed offline Studio runs used both original meshes.
Both runs passed all seven probes and produced identical exports.
