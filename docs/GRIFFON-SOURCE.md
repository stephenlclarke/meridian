# Griffon source evidence

SH071 retains its 1,644-triangle detailed and 596-triangle native visible
meshes. The original calibration is 60,000/6,221 metres per source unit. Both
measure 24.13 m wide, 9.03 m tall and 30.00 m long. The finite empty-dry
source records 30,998 kg.

A distinct 512-triangle collision artifact comes from the frozen source
tree in metres. The native artwork exceeds the 512-triangle collision
budget and remains the visible source. The archived collision artifact
preserves 17 of 19 original native parts exactly, including both gates and
their bridges. The main body and cockpit glazing account for the remaining
geometry changes.

Conservative distance bounds from the collision artifact to the original
native surface and back are 884 and 986 mm. The corresponding bounds against
the detailed surface are 975 and 1,990 mm. The 29 inspection supports have
upper stand-off bounds of 1,542 mm against detailed, 1,636 mm against the
original native and 1,946 mm against the collision artifact.

All seven guarded sweeps return the same values with the original meshes
and the archived collision artifact. Both inner gate paths retain 1,042 mm.
The paths above and below the forward bridge retain 1,009 and 1,288 mm.
Seven overlap witnesses are verified across all three meshes.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match, and all
seven artwork probes passed. The separately tested family recipe produced a
380-triangle Griffon candidate. The frozen facet pipeline has separate
provenance, and reproduction of the 512-triangle archive has not been
established from the frozen tree inputs. Gameplay qualification remains open.
