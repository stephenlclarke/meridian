# Hare Explorer source evidence

SH083 preserves 3,108 detailed and 184 native triangles in metres. The
detailed mesh measures 18.5 m wide, 9.442 m tall and 37.09 m long. The
finite empty-dry reference records 24,721 kg. The runtime catalogue
records flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication. The R3
design contract prescribes player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The 25 inspection supports use 20 mm rounding. Conservative stand-off
upper bounds are 2,092 mm against the detailed mesh and 2,616 mm against
the native mesh. Detailed vertex 1515 is more than 1,926 mm from every
native triangle. All seven guarded routes reproduce, from 770 mm of
clearance between the aft nozzles to 2,078 mm outside either forward boom.

Both meshes preserve the canopy-to-hull overlap and both wing mounts
overlap their wing and the hull. The detailed forward booms overlap the
hull, and the canopy ridge overlaps the canopy. The detailed nozzles have
two flush contacts with the hull at z = −15 m. The original native
geometry has certified gaps from the central hull of at least 800 mm to
each nozzle and 732 mm to each forward boom.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match. All seven
offline artwork probes passed.
