# Real energy recharge and paid Pulse ownership

## Problem

The initial modular slice can store a born Pulse but cannot purchase a normal Pulse replacement. Its energy recharge is also a fixed SH025 constant. The required modular system needs actual stock energy effects, source prices and compatible ownership, with paid firing receipts that survive later refits.

## Scope and acceptance

Implement [paid Pulse and energy criteria](ENERGY-PAID-PULSE.md): genuine wallet debits, retained born/paid IDs, exact fitting, stock rates/costs/prerequisites, complete save/cold refusal and publication failure. Keep original physical firing rules, shared pool and service bounds. All other equipment and all-hull binding remain open.
