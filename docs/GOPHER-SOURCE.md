metres. The detailed mesh measures 19.8 m wide, 15.8 m tall and 33.39 m
long. The finite empty-dry reference records 20,633 kg. The original
catalogue records player flight as unavailable and purchase pending
fabrication. The R3 design contract prescribes player flight and
blueprint fabrication.

The closed roll frame retains its openings around the occupied central
pressure hull. Eight local spans cover the frame with 128 shared seam
vertices. Thirty-one rounded supports cover both originals, with
conservative stand-off bounds of 2,952 mm detailed and 2,221 mm native.
Detailed vertex 2356 is more than 1,777 mm from every native triangle.

The upper and lower frame passages retain 312 and 858 mm of guarded
clearance. The lateral frame passages retain 593 mm on each side, and
the three exterior guards retain 1,229–1,344 mm. Both meshes retain nine
overlaps, including the bore head to the shaft and the frame stays to
both the frame and the pressure hull. The detailed canopy ridge
overlaps the canopy.

Both Developer ID–signed Studio exports and raw responses match. All
seven offline artwork probes passed. Studio duplicates seam vertices
to preserve each local support and the ordered coordinates of every
original triangle.
