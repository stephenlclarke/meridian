# Ox source evidence

SH099 preserves the original sand-graphite artwork in its 688-face
detailed mesh and 376-face native mesh. Both use the archived calibration
of 27,500/997 metres per source unit. The finite empty-dry reference
records 524,258 kg. The runtime catalogue records flight as unavailable
and purchase pending fabrication. The R3 design contract specifies
player flight and blueprint fabrication.

Both reference meshes have 12 closed components. All 13 measured overlap
pairs reproduce in both, including both deck braces against each deck
and the crossmember against the cockpit glazing. The deck bodies are
vertically separated by at least 7,723 mm. The glazing sits at least
441 mm above the upper deck body. Each luminous recess is at least
137 mm aft of its casing.

The 12 supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guards provide 2,198–4,300 mm
of clearance. The full centre route through the service gap retains
2,198 mm, and both forward offset routes retain 3,704 mm. The largest
distance from a detailed vertex to the native surface is about 287 mm.
Every native vertex lies on the detailed surface.

Both Developer ID–signed offline Studio runs used the two original
meshes, passed all seven probes and produced identical exports.
