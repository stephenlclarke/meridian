# Saiga source evidence

SH061 retains the original 5,776-triangle detailed mesh and 1,720-triangle
native mesh. Both use the archived calibration of exactly 20 metres per source
unit. The archived Decimal80 estimate records 284,291 kg of empty dry mass.
The archive records native qualification as unrun.

The separate project-authored native appearance meets the 512-triangle
inspection limit. It keeps twelve original parts byte for byte, including six
structural members and the six outer-facing blind wells. Eight curved parts
retain their original longitudinal stations, and both collars retain their
through-holes and original axis extrema. The candidate omits six inboard blind
wells, original native parts 3, 4, 7, 10, 14 and 15. Their measured
mouth-to-floor upper bounds are 1,041 mm. Both original meshes retain all their
features.

The largest archived-native-to-candidate deviations occur at the omitted
inboard wells. Across the two collars, the upper bounds are 1,499 mm from
candidate to archive and 1,734 mm in the reverse direction. The candidate
preserves all six global native axis extrema. In 16 sampled directions per
orthographic view, the maximum native silhouette offsets are 657 mm in XY and
zero in XZ and YZ. The corresponding offsets from the detailed mesh are 657 mm,
zero and 526 mm.

The bundled collision data assigns all 6,288 detailed and candidate faces to
72 local supports. Eight sectors per collar overlap on 256 original boundary
vertices. All seven guarded clearance values match the original-mesh
configuration. The forward and aft collar centers have 2,256 mm and 1,499 mm of
clearance. The two inner forward routes have 511 mm and 515 mm. The independent
negative control confirms that the original central web remains occupied in all
three meshes. The bundled support stand-off upper bounds are 2,173 mm from
the detailed mesh, 2,556 mm from the archived native mesh and 4,671 mm from the
candidate. The largest candidate stand-off is at an omitted inboard well.

Both fit calculations use the original MacBook Air triangulations for all 72
supports. Each replay checks the original vertex indices, metre coordinates,
and outward triangles, including vertices shared by adjacent collar sectors,
then recalculates the distance bounds. The [inspection criteria](../integration/sh061-saiga/CRITERIA.json)
record the original measurement script hashes and the triangulation capture.

Developer ID–signed Hull Studio produced two byte-identical offline artwork
exports and responses for the 5,776/512 definition and recorded three exterior
probes. The bundled collision verifier checks all seven routes, including those
through the collars. The original catalogue records player flight and purchase
eligibility through blueprint fabrication. The Meridian source module exposes
