# Shikra source evidence

SH094 preserves the 5,396-face detailed and 1,404-face native originals
with cobalt-cream artwork and calibration of 1,250/131 metres per source
unit. Its finite empty-dry reference records 39,320 kg. The runtime
catalogue records flight as unavailable and purchase pending fabrication.
The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint fabrication.

The separate 512-face inspection candidate retains all 41 native parts,
including all 20 blind service-well parts. It keeps eleven solid parts
byte-identical and selects original vertices for the other 30. The
candidate preserves the original native extent on each axis and the
material inventory of every part. Rebuilt surfaces approximate the
original shapes and UV mappings. The two original visual streams retain
their original geometry, materials and UVs.

All 18 measured native overlaps survive at the same witness points,
including the four diagonal spars and the cockpit crossmember. A separate
candidate survey observed the same 18 pairs. The candidate retains at least
47 mm of longitudinal separation between each aft casing and its luminous
recess. Conservative surface-distance upper bounds are 1,243 mm from the
archived native mesh to the candidate and 994 mm in reverse.

The 91 source supports use 20 mm rounding. Seven guards pass with
375–2,405 mm of clearance, including both openings between diagonal spars.
Two Developer ID–signed Studio runs used the original detailed mesh and
the 512-face candidate. Both passed all seven probes and produced
byte-identical exports.
