## Current candidate

- Buy one actual item in Equipment → E.C.M. utility at an admitted berth with displayed TL 5 or greater and 2,100.0 CR. Store and reinstall the same identity; no duplicate purchase or automatic grant.
- Press **K** in flight for one manual field. The original E key is not reused because Meridian already uses E for roll. The command spends 22 energy once, does not change heat or laser cadence, and does not advance the physical clock. The field lasts 32 accepted physical ticks in the same visit. It ends actual existing standard projectiles immediately and also ends new local standard projectiles emitted before expiry. This is an `Ecm` terminal outcome, not an impact, interception beam, reward or ammunition refund. The HUD shows ECM FITTED, ECM ACTIVE or ECM COOLDOWN. The activation and the existing-projectile outcomes publish together through the real profile save; a failed publication changes neither.
- ECM and EMP remain separate. An EMP-disabled ship cannot activate ECM. An accepted ECM cancels an unfinished EMP charge and refuses a new EMP trigger while the field remains active.
- The activation ledger binds the fitted item, owner, hull, system, visit and accepted clocks. The shared resource replay checks the cost. The independent EMP meter replay checks every activation after that physical step's release or recovery, including the final clock of an electrical disable interval.
- Actual launch steps retain the initial native station geometry, controls and opening traffic scenes. The resource journal independently marks the accepted transition to local Flight. The navigation-owned jump journal independently seals the actual accepted departure, source and destination, visit and both clocks, fitted duration and any prior same-clock ECM sequence. This is cross-checked with the separate local ECM record, pending or completed travel, and the independently marked cancellation berth. A retained activation cannot use a Launching or travelling clock, even if another receipt tries to shift the cancelled departure or rewrite command order.
- The existing tagged v17 `MECM0002` extension preserves the local admission and timing records. The new `MECM0003` extension also records actual field opening and cold boundaries, ordinary emission order and projectile terminations. A cold Continue retains past outcomes and remaining cooldown time, but consumes live-effect authority. A later projectile survives this time-only cooldown unless a new valid field is activated. V2 saves remain timer-only; they do not acquire retroactive effects. Genuine non-ECM saves retain their prior encoding. Independent content markers bind both extensions.
- XYZZY initial activation enables automatic intent, as in the original. The separate guarded menu entries Fit E.C.M. only and Toggle automatic E.C.M. retain provenance and do not grant cash, fuel or unrelated items. The latter is a setting only. No automatic emission receipt is accepted until a real incoming ordnance owner exists.

## Acceptance and limits

The candidate authors paid acquisition/store/refit/rollback, shared mount and secret guard, strict energy edge, same-clock repeat refusal, 32-tick expiry, paused/stale refusal, retained history corruption and cold continuation checks. A normal paid path covers an actual live practice missile, an actual newborn round during the field, a round emitted after cold Continue, and a later new activation. Ordinary laser commands test the shared energy balance and both same-clock orders. Desktop cases cover the actual menu, K dispatch, failed save publication and retained projectile outcome. A separate real NPC Pulse encounter exercises cancelling an unfinished EMP charge without releasing it, followed by cold validation. The retained electrical replay has a labelled component fixture derived from a real pulse to test exact disabling and recovery clocks. Public launch and jump tests retain the independent local and navigation admission evidence. Rust and native preview remain unqualified until the owning platforms run the exact source.

For native preview use a disposable commander only. Verify the ordinary paid equipment and price; for a separate fast test, activate XYZZY, store one ordinary utility and fit E.C.M. Consent to the actual practice at Avenor, launch, lock and arm a real practice missile, fire it and press K. Observe the energy reduction, ECM ACTIVE, the actual projectile disappearance and the termination notice. Cold Continue during the field retains the existing result but shows ECM COOLDOWN until expiry. Do not claim destruction, warning or interception of an incoming missile during this preview; no such game object is currently produced. Full playable ECM acceptance still needs a real NPC-produced incoming projectile, incoming danger presentation and automatic policy acting on that event. Audio/announcement and broader ordnance variants remain outside this candidate.
