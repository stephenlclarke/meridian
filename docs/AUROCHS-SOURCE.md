# Aurochs source evidence

SH092 preserves the navy-copper artwork in the original 2,504-face detailed
mesh and 720-face native mesh, calibrated at 40,000/4,221 metres per source
unit. The finite empty-dry reference records 37,180 kg. The original runtime
catalogue records player flight as unavailable and purchase pending
fabrication. The R3 design contract specifies player flight and blueprint
fabrication.

The pinned recipe makes a separate 444-face inspection candidate with all
18 original parts. Eleven parts retain their exact geometry and artwork
attributes, including eight blind service wells, the cockpit crossmember and
both drive supports. The candidate retains all 12 measured original overlaps
and at least 284 mm of vertical separation between the two staggered cargo
capsule bodies.

The conservative surface bounds are 1,242 mm from the archived native mesh
to the candidate and 1,250 mm in reverse. The corresponding bounds between
the detailed mesh and candidate are 2,371 mm and 1,499 mm. The 38 local
supports contain the three meshes with 20 mm rounding. Their conservative
stand-off upper bounds are 2,524 mm against either original and 2,882 mm
against the candidate.

Seven guards provide 772–1,373 mm of clearance, including an upper aft
approach and two forward routes alongside the staggered cargo capsules. The
forward routes begin ahead of the propulsion casings. Both offline
Developer ID–signed Studio runs passed all seven probes with the original
detailed mesh and the 444-face candidate. The two exports match byte for
byte.
