# Seriema Courier source evidence

SH063 preserves its 1,656-triangle original detailed artwork and 608-triangle
original native artwork, including their visible render streams. Both originals
use the frozen calibration of 75/11 metres per source unit. The finite empty-dry
source is `finite-intrinsic-empty-seriema-courier` at 14,539 kg.

The separate archived collision artifact has 296 vertices and 512 triangles
in metres, with all 20 original parts. Its archived hash and source lineage
identify the exact geometry used by the inspection module. Running the pinned
creator recipe with both original tests passing yielded 272 vertices and 464
faces. A variant with the same counts as the archive differed in the vertex,
face or UV streams. The archived collision geometry is identified by its
frozen bytes, with the mismatch recorded alongside it.

The collision artifact preserves the original vertex, face, material and UV
streams for 16 parts after calibration. Against the archived native mesh, its
directed surface upper bounds are 934 mm from collision to archive and 900 mm
from archive to collision. The corresponding bounds against the detailed mesh
are 984 mm and 1,235 mm. All six axis extrema match both original meshes. In
16 sampled directions per orthographic view, silhouette offsets are zero
against the original native mesh and reach 48 mm in XY against the detail.
The collision support stand-off upper bound is 1,580 mm against each of the
three meshes.

The bundled 30-group collision geometry retains all seven original guarded
clearance values of 448–629 mm. Both passages alongside the full blade rail
retain 448 mm. The independent source verifier checks four source joints in
all three meshes, including the connection from the blade to the main body.

Developer ID–signed Hull Studio produced two byte-identical offline
inspection exports and responses containing 1,656 detailed artwork triangles
and the separate 512 collision triangles. The bundled native visual stream
carries the original 608-triangle visible mesh. The frozen runtime catalogue lists
Seriema Courier as unflyable and unavailable for normal purchase. The original
R3 contract records blueprint fabrication as the intended purchase policy.
