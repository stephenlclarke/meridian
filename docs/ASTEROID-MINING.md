# Asteroid mining core

This main-based background slice implements an authored Avenor belt provider, shared physical geometry, finite material depletion, a World mining transaction and native cargo/market conservation. It is **development source, not playable acceptance**. The genuine paid Mining Laser producer is frozen in equipment commit `b1bed892ba4b37a5996b2c964e0c7fc532c1c2fb`; its exact serial composition patch and five joined tests accompany this delivery outside the repository. No public tool constructor, starter-Pulse substitution or granted tool is supplied here. Execution of the actual paid-tool positive/unfit/refit/changed-item tests, the final equipment/power join, chart controls, rendered bodies and owned compiler/native qualification remain required before the feature can ship. HUD, docked menus and modular equipment retain preview priority. No Lua/controller dependency or mission integration is introduced.

## Player behavior and authored content

A fresh mining-format commander has one Avenor ore-belt segment at galaxy 0, system 7. Its centre is `(4000, 0, 8000)` in the same station-local frame as actual flight; the first rock is at `(4000, 0, 6000)`. Twelve fixed ordinal addresses and integer offsets form a sparse local annulus. This is newly authored Meridian placement, not a claim of matching L337's sun-centred generation or a new cosmological orbit simulation. The field is off the ordinary station launch/approach corridor.

`World::mining_destination(selectedSystem)` is the authoritative chart/destination query, including from another current system. `World::mining_belt_snapshot()` returns actual local body addresses, positions, six world-space vertices per rock, native commodity quantities, depletion and visible front contact. Use `ASTEROID_FACES` with those vertices for rendering. Chart and flight presentation must read these values; copied coordinates or independent random placement are not accepted. No destination is reported for an absent provider or an unsupported system. The renderer and chart join is still pending.

Mining requires the real paid Mining Laser fitted to the current craft's front laser slot. The source equipment facts are 3,300 CR, required TL 6, damage 50 and 0.3-second discharge interval; equipment ownership, payment, displaced-Pulse storage and fit remain with the modular equipment/account owner. No separate mining utility slot or wallet is added. The existing ship energy/heat/cooldown store is used: opening heat at most 230, strictly more than one energy, one energy and ten heat spent, then 18 active ticks of cooldown. Storing Pulse and fitting Mining must retain this same resource pool in the final equipment join.

The player must approach, slow to at most two game-speed units, aim at the actual nearest rock surface within 800 station-space units and have a clear first-contact ray. Pause, transit, invalid tool/fit/epoch, collision, obstruction, cooldown, heat, energy, depletion and cargo capacity refuse before a stroke or cargo change. No target, ray, damage, ore quantity, price or success flag comes from the UI. Rock, station, practice and live-projectile occlusion share the World scene query. The player hull is stopped conservatively against the identical closed rock mesh, with the existing continuous rotation/translation envelope.

Each accepted stroke transfers exactly one declared native commodity unit into the sole account and reduces that body's finite reserve by one. This is an intentional Meridian work/yield rule, not L337 fragmentation/damage parity: one Minerals/Alloys unit is a tonne and one Gold/Platinum unit is a kilogram, following existing market units. Initial reserves are fixed per body and visible in the snapshot. There is no cash award, loose-ore respawn, scoop grant or mission reward. Existing hold and tutorial reservations apply. Ordinary market selling credits only the normal real station bid. Pulse and missiles stop at the physical rock but do not mine it; exhausted rocks remain physical collision/render bodies.

## Authority, custody and persistence

`MiningToolAuthority` is opaque outside core. Its genuine equipment producer must validate paid ownership, compatibility, current fit and usability, then bind owner, hull, account revision, current item, freight epoch and the actual live World's weak scope. It also supplies core's real read-only fitted-mining-item lookup. `World::request_mining` re-queries that item before querying the work scene. Any account/fit change invalidates the lease. The live scope is never encoded; each decode/restore creates a new one, so even two independent Continues of one file with identical numeric IDs/epochs cannot share a capability. The separate composition patch binds this producer to G3's actual `fitted_mining_tool` and `paid_fitted_mining_item`, and routes resource preparation through `prepare_mining_resources`. The isolated main-based branch cannot mint a tool until that real join is applied.

The transaction stages depletion, a copied account candidate, actual power/heat/cooldown and an emission before its first mutation. All subsequent assignments are infallible. The sole account gains the commodity and one revision; cash, fuel, existing gear and market stock stay unchanged. Global save validation reconciles material extraction against account cargo and market sales, and includes mining strokes in revision/discharge accounting. Buying or selling ore does not replenish the source body. Resetting the depletion ledger after ore is sold fails the joint conservation checks.

The isolated candidate writes explicit World version 12 with a mining rules identifier and a 25-byte body ledger: one presence byte and twelve remaining/extracted pairs. Its content fingerprint includes canonical body identities, positions, vertices/faces, commodities, initial quantities and work rules, so changed content cannot silently move or replenish a saved body. Every enabled body's remaining plus extracted quantity equals its declared initial reserve. Versions 4 through 11 retain their existing explicit readers and content fingerprints and have an absent belt with zero mining history; Continue does not invent a fresh field or migrate a player into mining. Those profiles can be written in the new format while retaining the absent-provider state. No owned save or installed preview is opened or changed. Root owns the final combined equipment/mining format number; this isolated version is not a reservation against other owner work.

Malformed presence, conservation, unsupported IDs, mining without account/discharge, intersecting hulls, corrupt lengths/checksums or unsupported versions refuse without fallback or repair. All ordinary saves include the same ledger. Beam/projectile contacts and their save replay use the actual body source system; travel away from Avenor does not erase or reinterpret a historical rock impact.

## Validation and integration requirements

Thirteen new Rust tests are authored and **UNRUN**. They cover closed mesh/contact identity, range/depletion/roundtrip and legacy absence; actual public flight approach, rock-ray Pulse obstruction and swept collision; navigation/cold snapshot conservation; foreign/stale/paused/docked refusals and cross-Continue scope rejection; malformed/unbacked depletion refusal; native cargo, hold/tutorial reservations and sale conservation; and the exact mining energy/heat/cooldown boundary. Internal component and negative fixtures are labelled and do not establish a paid-tool journey.

Five additional paid-tool tests are authored **UNRUN** in the separate serial composition patch. They cover actual profitable commerce and paid purchase; shared resource debit and cooldown refusal; foreign Continue, restore, pause and unfit; genuine refit and a second paid item; depletion across every cold Continue followed by ordinary sale, physical return and no regeneration; and public paid purchases filling the hold before a no-shot refusal. They use the actual G3 source, with no granted tool, seeded cash, pose edit or new lint suppression. Their compilation and execution require the joined equipment source. Mining Lasers have no finite ammunition or tool durability in this scope; finite source depletion and actual resource admission are the work limits. Root must combine the actual G3 producer and shared resource readout, Researcher's chart/selection UI, G1's renderer/HUD and the explicit codec delta. Minimal desktop changes in this packet only handle the new contact/refusal variants. Existing and affected core/host tests, strict Clippy, coverage, independent review and actual native approach/work/save/quit/Continue are required on that exact candidate. No compiler, Lua VM, game process or new Rust helper has been executed by this author; formatting/document checks remain separate from runtime results.

L337's owned `MiningWorkTool.swift`, `MiningLedger.swift`, `AsteroidBeltDefinition.swift` and `MiningFields.swift` at `a11fe16924d87d06021816f9037952afcbac08db` informed the work guard, finite depletion and native-unit requirements. The current fixed belt geometry, placement and per-stroke yield are Meridian design choices. Existing imported hull/station/commodity notices remain intact. No external game asset or third-party mining framework is introduced.
