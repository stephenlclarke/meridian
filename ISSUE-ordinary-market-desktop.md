# Ordinary market controls

The playable desktop exposes only Food transactions even though the original economy contains seventeen native commodity IDs. The next coherent economy slice must make each commodity selectable in its original display order and show the actual finite market, wallet, cargo and reservations without granting mission or station authority.

## Required behavior

M opens or closes the market; bracket keys select in the original display order. G/H submit one native-unit buy/sell command to World. Food is the initial selection. Kilograms and grams retain the original zero-tonne hold rule. Identified freight and actual reservations remain owned by the core. Changing panels invalidates pending mission confirmations.

Keep the selected row and controls readable within the existing 640x360 fourteen-line HUD budget. Prices and quantities stay exact. Include glyphs for the new controls and selection marker. Preserve the installed v1 preview while the joined v2 core, content and desktop artifact is reviewed and qualified.
