# The Unwritten Route clue journal

Press **Shift+J** from normal flight or the station to open the clue journal. The journal shows progress against the 64 original distinct source clues and lists the clues the current commander has earned. Escape closes it. Unmodified **J** keeps its jump control.

Meridian joins the first earned receipts from authenticated Consented Freight, Flight Reception, Safe Gunnery and Navigation and Trade records. Accepting a job, repeating a completed family or visiting a station cannot add a new clue on its own. Continue rebuilds the view from the same saved receipts. The journal adds no save fields or stored claim.

The journal hides later bearings and the craft reveal. The source requires all 64 clues before a player can ask the AI at an actual PLUGH station to decode the traces. The ordered flight trail and Medusa encounter follow. The source requires the player to return the same intact lattice before PLUGH can grant a separate, optional paid purchase. The mission uses its own limited fit. The shop uses the paid stock fit. Legal maximum equipment comes after that purchase. The source provides no independent paid or preview-flight entitlement. This change ships the journal interaction. PLUGH decoding, the ordered trail, physical capture, receiving and ship acquisition remain open.

The [source receipt](SOURCE.json) pins the original approved clue catalogue, story sequence and controlling Medusa configuration. Catalogue entries are requirements. Only the owning Meridian completion reducers can publish the corresponding earned receipts. The source assets retain their [original licence](LICENSE).

## Acceptance

Open the journal in a fresh normal commander and check the zero count. Complete and receive one supported activity, then confirm that the journal shows the earned clue and still requires all 64 distinct clues. Save, quit and Continue. Verify that the same source clue remains visible. Check that closing the journal leaves the commander at the same location and that unmodified J still goes to the usual jump handler.
